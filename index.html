Model and units

The simulation uses Newtonian gravity for two point masses. The mass ratio is q = m/M. When the masses are comparable, both move noticeably around their common centre of mass. The value q = 0 is the test-particle limit.

The units are chosen so that G = M = R = 1. Separations are measured in R, speed in √(GM/R) and time in √(R³/GM). In these units, the gravitational parameter for relative motion is μ = 1 + q, corresponding to G(M + m).

The initial speed is the speed of m relative to M. Energy and angular momentum are given per unit of reduced mass, in units of GM/R and √(GMR). Following the central body changes the view; the calculation still includes both bodies.

The markers are sized for visibility and are not to scale. The model has no physical surfaces or collision radii. Changing an initial condition restarts the simulation with the new value.

The dashed line shows the predicted orbit, while the solid trail shows the motion so far. Open and very elongated orbits may extend beyond the view. Select “Fit orbit” to include the bodies' current positions.
