# Project logos

The Norwegian and English logos share exactly the same geometric symbol.
Use the SVG files when possible: they scale without loss of quality and
the wordmarks are stored as outlines, so no font installation is needed.

The PNG files are raster exports of the corresponding SVG files.

## Geometry

The symbol represents an orbit viewed in its own plane. Its semiaxes are
`a = 500` and `b = 210`. The sun is at the left focus,
`(-sqrt(a² - b²), 0)`, and the planet is at
`(a cos θ, b sin θ)`, with `θ = -35°`. The entire symbol is rotated
by `-12°` and translated by `(700, 340)`.

The ellipse is an SVG `ellipse` element. A small transparent gap around
the planet keeps the contour legible. The body markers are schematic;
their radii do not represent physical sizes.

The shared symbol was developed from the selected ImageGen concept and
then reconstructed geometrically. The original raster drafts are not used
as production assets. The SVGs are generated by `tools/build_logos.py`
with Python, fonttools and a locally supplied bold TrueType font. These
are optional authoring tools; the simulator has no new dependencies.
